# AERON VANTA X1 — original aircraft asset

- File: `drone.glb` (glTF 2.0 binary)
- Source: original procedural geometry authored specifically for this AERON concept experience.
- Generator: `blender/aeron_x1.py` (Blender 4.0.2).
- Third-party mesh or texture assets: none.
- Geometry: 140 authored components, batched into 16 runtime nodes; 67,232 triangles and 12 PBR materials.
- Animation: four independent, correctly centered rotor nodes (`Rotor_FL`, `Rotor_FR`, `Rotor_BL`, `Rotor_BR`).
- Details: beveled airframe, folding-arm hinges, motor bells, swept propellers, camera gimbal and optical elements, obstacle sensors, vents, landing gear and navigation lights.
- SHA-256: `f1b92c160a45938349fb0e8baf3b1a926b2919adc22ae7570b961f2037d4c78e`.

The earlier Poly Pizza prototype asset is no longer used by the site. Its only
backup is kept outside the public directory as `blender/drone-lowpoly-backup.glb`.
